Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

Mothligth Clips is a personal tool used by its owner to publish the owner's own video clips to the owner's own social media accounts. It does not offer a service to the public.

What we access: when the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives basic profile information (such as display name) and permission to upload videos to that account. Nothing else is accessed.

How it is used: this access is used only to publish videos the owner chooses to post. We do not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties.

Storage: login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's app settings.
